LLM Book Club — Setup Steps
============================

1. Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "B:\projects\claude\llm-book-club\setup\01_install_docker.ps1"

2. Restart your computer.

3. After restart, open PowerShell and run:
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "B:\projects\claude\llm-book-club\setup\02_start_app.ps1"

4. Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.

5. Search D:\books on the shelf and click Read. EPUB, Kindle, CBZ, PDF,
   and TXT files open without AI setup. Use "Prepare AI" when you want a
   grounded discussion for an EPUB, PDF, or TXT book.

That's it. Come back to Claude Code and say "continue" to pick up where we left off.
